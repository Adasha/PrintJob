# Lookup lists

Keep the short lists on the Settings sheet that the Papers and Printers
sheets choose from.

## The lists

Three tables sit to the right of the global settings on the **Settings**
sheet:

| List | Used by | Columns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paper stock types** | **Paper type** on Papers | Paper type, Active |
| **Standard paper sizes (mm)** | **Std. size** on Papers, **Max sheet size** on Printers, **Sheet size** on jobs | Size name, Width mm, Height mm |
| **Consumable types** | **Consumable type** on Printers and Consumables | Consumable type, Active |

## Add an entry

1. Click the **+** button beside the list. A new row appears at the bottom.
2. Type the entry. For a size, type its name, width and height in
   millimetres, such as `B2`, `500`, `707`.
3. For a type, leave **Active** as Yes.

You can also add a row the usual Excel way: press Tab in the last cell of the
table.

## Remove an entry

1. Click a cell in the row you want to remove.
2. Click the **-** button beside the list.
3. Check the row shown and click **Yes**.

## Things to know

- To stop offering a paper or consumable type, set **Active** to No rather
  than removing it. Papers and printers that use it keep working.
- Removing or changing an entry does not change jobs already recorded.
- Standard sizes have no Active column. Keep sizes that printers or papers
  still use.
