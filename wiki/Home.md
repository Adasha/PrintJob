# PrintJob

PrintJob is an Excel workbook for logging print jobs in a university print
bureau and working out what each one costs. Each print room has its own sheet
of jobs. The workbook prices every job from your printers, papers and
consumables, and adds them all up on the Summary and Reports sheets.

This wiki is for the people who use the workbook at the desk and the person who
sets it up. It covers Excel for Microsoft 365 on Windows and on a Mac.

## Which version

These pages describe workbook version 0.11.5. To see which version you have,
open the **Settings** sheet and click **About**. What changed in each version
is in [Release notes](Release-Notes).

> **Mac:** use version 0.11.1 or later. In earlier versions, Export and Backup
