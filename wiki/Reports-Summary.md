# Summary

See the totals for the whole workbook at a glance, and price a job before you
print it.

## Read the totals

Open the **Summary** sheet. It updates as jobs are added, with nothing to
refresh.

- **Totals** (row 24): the number of jobs, and the Gross, Disregarded,
  Chargeable, Paid and Unpaid amounts across every print room.
- **The table below** has one row per print room, printer and paper stock,
  with the jobs, quantity, area and each cost.
- **By department** (top right): jobs and costs for each department on the
  Departments sheet, with **Everyone else** for the rest. It shows up to 14
  departments; Reports has the full list.

Quantity and area count every job, including disregarded ones. Only the
money columns split out what is disregarded.

## Price a job before printing

1. In **Plan a print job**, choose the **Printer** and the **Paper**.
2. Fill in what the paper needs: **Roll length** (and **Print width mm** if
   narrower than the roll) for roll paper, or **Sheet size** for a sheet the
   student brought.
3. Read the **Estimated cost**: paper, ink and overall.

Nothing is recorded yet.

## Add the planned job to a room

1. Under **Add to print room**, choose the room.
2. Click **Add to print room**.
3. On the room's sheet, fill in the student, technician and anything else the
   job needs.

The room must have the planned printer ticked in **Select printers...**. If
the plan is not complete, the workbook says what is missing.

## Things to know

- A paper shown in the Type column as **(not in Papers)** has been renamed or
  removed since its jobs were recorded. The costs are still right.
- **Hide settings sheets** hides the setup sheets from the tab bar, to keep
  the workbook tidy at the desk. It is not a lock. See
  [Protection and hidden sheets](Admin-Protection-and-Hidden-Sheets).
- **Refresh Locations** and **Check workbook** are also on this sheet. See
  [Print rooms](Setup-Print-Rooms) and
  [Validation and status](Jobs-Validation-and-Status).
